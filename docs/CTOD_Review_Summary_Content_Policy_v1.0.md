# CTOD Review Summary Content Policy v1.0

The finalized review summary is a decision-oriented summary, not a transcript of the full review record.

## Performance summary
- Key Strengths includes only Exceptional ratings.
- Development Areas includes only Needs Improvement and Unsatisfactory ratings.
- Exceeds Expectations and Meets Expectations remain preserved in the finalized review history and analytics but are intentionally omitted from the printed/PDF summary unless a future approved report mode explicitly requests full detail.
- Included performance items retain the question, rating, selected reason, and manager note.

## Coaching summary
- Coaching history remains fully preserved in CTOD.
- The finalized review summary shows only the number of coaching items included and a count by coaching type.
- Individual coaching notes, reasons, expected outcomes, and historical detail are not printed on the standard summary.

## Report presentation
- Report-only CSS classes must be namespaced with `ctod-report-` to prevent customer workspace styles from leaking into PDF/print output.
- The canonical adaptive portrait report remains the single source for on-screen summary, Download PDF, and Print Portrait Report.
- Summary content may expand to additional portrait pages when necessary, but concise filtering should keep ordinary positive reviews short and reserve page space for meaningful development and coaching signals.
